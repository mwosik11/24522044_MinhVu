# AI Audit Report – Lab 02

AI was useful for solving immediate problems, providing coding suggestions, and helping with debugging. However, the development process still required continuous human supervision and verification.

One major limitation was the tendency to implement solutions step by step without a clear overall plan. This sometimes resulted in inconsistent code, a lack of coordination between components, and minor bugs that required additional prompts and debugging.

- **Exercise 1:** The development process was relatively smooth, with few significant issues.
- **Exercise 2:** More problems emerged as the implementation became complex. Additional prompts were needed to identify issues and find appropriate solutions.
- **Exercise 3:** The process was more stable, with only a few minor issues that were resolved through debugging and testing.

Overall, AI can accelerate software development, but it should not be relied on without supervision. A clear plan, consistent architecture, and systematic testing are essential to ensure code quality and reliability.
